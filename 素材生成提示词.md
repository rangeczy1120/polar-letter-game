# ImageGen 素材提示词

生成方式：Codex 内置 ImageGen，每个用途分别生成。透明参数仅用于雪花粒子；原图尺寸为工具实际输出尺寸。

## snow_ground_basecolor

Seamless tileable PBR basecolor texture for a 3D game ground: fine natural powder snow, tiny ice grains, subtly uneven microstructure, cool blue-white polar night color, orthographic overhead, diffuse even light, no shadows, no objects, no text.

## ice_lake_basecolor

Seamless tileable PBR basecolor texture for frozen lake ice, solid translucent pale cyan-blue ice with subtle cloudy zones, no liquid water, overhead orthographic, diffuse unlit even illumination, no shadows or text.

## ice_lake_cracks

Tileable game ice fracture detail texture: hairline irregular natural frozen ice fissures, bright fine blue-white lines on dark neutral background, overhead orthographic, evenly distributed, no objects, no text.

## ice_lake_frost

Tileable fine frost detail texture for a frozen lake: delicate natural fern-shaped ice frost crystals, sparse pale cyan-white frost on a dark neutral field, top down, no objects, no text.

## wood_basecolor

Seamless tileable PBR basecolor texture of warmly brown weathered cabin timber planks, natural grain and subtle small scratches, restrained seams, straight orthographic front elevation, no objects, no text, evenly lit, no shadows.

## crystal_detail

Seamless tileable texture for inside an existing 3D ice crystal: fine frozen hairline fractures, microscopic bubbles and fern frost in translucent cyan and pale blue ice, orthographic unlit, no object silhouette or scene, no text.

## aurora_background

Wide panoramic aurora-only sky texture, deep navy polar night sky with delicate teal green and muted lavender aurora ribbons, naturally subtle smooth gradients and sparse stars; no landscape or ground, no objects, no text.

## snow_particle

A single delicate softly luminous white ice snowflake particle sprite centered, isolated, thin intricate crystalline six-fold pattern with softly feathered edges, genuinely transparent background, no text, no black background.


## polar_environment

Stylized-concept; panoramic equirectangular environment map for an atmospheric 3D polar night game. Deep navy starry sky with very subtle teal aurora and low diffuse warm gold glow at horizon, gentle snow plains far below, no cabin, no characters, no text, soft even atmospheric illumination, suitable for reflection on translucent ice. Full 360 degree seamless horizontal wrap, 2:1 wide.
